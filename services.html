Travel documentation

Help with the steps beyond the booking.

Document attestation

Attestation may be required to confirm the authenticity of educational, personal or commercial documents. The steps depend on the document, destination and purpose; we help you understand and coordinate the applicable process.

VFS appointment assistance

We provide appointment guidance, scheduling assistance, document-preparation guidance and appointment-related support where relevant.

Appointment availability is controlled by the relevant system or application centre and cannot be guaranteed by BeyondTripz.

OK to Board

Some airlines or destinations may require an OK to Board update after visa issuance. Where applicable, we help check the process, identify likely documents and coordinate the request. Requirements vary by airline, route and destination.
