Typical documents

What you may be asked to prepare

Common categories include identity and passport documents, photographs, travel plans, accommodation, financial evidence, employment or study records, invitation or sponsorship documents and supporting letters. The exact list varies.

Common mistakes

Small inconsistencies can create avoidable questions.

  • Choosing the wrong visa category
  • Incomplete or inconsistent forms
  • Missing supporting documents
  • Unclear travel purpose
  • Applying without checking validity rules
  • Relying on outdated online information
